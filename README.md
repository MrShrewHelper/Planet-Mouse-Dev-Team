## Game
#### We are thinking about making a RPG game. This might be similar to a choose your own adventure game. We could have different classes, weapons, storylines ect. This will most likely not be very advanced. You will use WASD or arrow key to move to character, and the mouse to interact with things. Lots of people who like to play games might use this. This game could cater to lots of game styles.
