# Planet-Mouse-Dev-Team
## Base Game Idea
#### We are thinking about making a RPG game. This might be similar to a choose your own adventure game. We could have different classes, weapons, storylines ect. This will most likely not be very advanced. You will use WASD or arrow key to move to character, and the mouse to interact with things. Lots of people who like to play games might use this. This game could cater to lots of game styles. It also will be fun to play test. We might might try and make it so you can do two player or even online parties to go through the game.
## Steps
### If the player presses the WASD keys, the character moves. If the player clicks on something, they can interact with stuff.

## Classes
### Warrior
#### Description: This is your basic adventurer. Basic damage, basic defence Items and armor. Is best at melee damage and frontline fighting. An all round good class for learning the game.
#### Weapons That Can Be Used By This Class:
##### Note: All weapons can be found in Legendary forms which are better than basic weapons, but they are very rare.
#### Sword: Simple adventurer weapon basic stats, no downsides
#### Claymore: BIG Sword. High attack damage, but a slower attack speed. Great For experienced adventurers who are not afraid to take damage and like charging into a fight.
#### Spear/Glaive ( Note: These are basically same weapon, but a glaive is used more like a sword to slash, while spears jab we have decided to group these weapons together) Great for a front line combatant who enjoys attacking from a distance and not taking as much damage. Best for experienced players. Trades off attack speed for range and damage.
#### War Hammer: A hammer which is great for AOE damage and is excellent at clearing crowds. Probably the best weapon for a beginner. Trades off attack speed for damage.
#### Katana: A VERY fast sword which is great for close range combat as a front line combatant. Trades off range for Attack Speed.
#### Broadsword: BIG FLAT HEAVY Sword. Basically the same as the Claymore, but with more damage and less Attack Speed.
#### Bow and Arrow: Normal Bow and arrow. Arrows are not infinite

### Ranger
#### Bow and Arrow: Normal Bow and arrow. Arrows are not infinite. It does ranged damage. The longer you hold the further the arrow reaches and the more damage it does. Is NOT spammable if you spam you get two weak arrows a second. Good for attacking without getting in danger.
#### Crossbow: Same thing as bow and arrow except it can be preloaded. Trades range and damage for attack speed. Excelent secondary weapon
#### Longbow: Is a normal bow but trades attack speed for range and damage. Excelent at dealing high damage at far ranges.
#### Firework: Is a firework. Explosion, fire, and AOE damage. Compatable with bows
#### Blowgun: Fires silent darts. Trades damage and range for attack speed
#### Hatchet: Melee weapon for the ranger. Acts as a sword with less range and damage. Great for close combat if you suck at aiming
#### AWP: A bit out of place. It's a sniper rifle. Very rare very rare but one shots anything. Ammo is also rare. Bullets are hitscan. 
#### Harpoon: Mobility option. Shoot it at a wall to get pulled to the location you shot. Shoot it at a person to drag that person to you.
#### Flintlock: Very primitive gun. Trades raw damage for attack speed. Very very slow attack speed and very very long reload speed.
#### Musket: A flintlock with higher reload speed but higher damage and more speed.
#### Smoke bomb: Bomb that emits smoke good for escaping best paired with harpoon.
